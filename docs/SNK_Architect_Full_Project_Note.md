## 1. Project Vision

SNK Architect aims to help clients understand what a proposed home, room, or building could look like before construction or renovation. The platform will bring together design concepts, 2D plans, 3D visualization, construction timeline concepts, cost estimates, building information, and immersive previews.

**Core promise:** “Visualize your future home before it is built.”

Important: a photorealistic visualization is a proposed design, not a guarantee of the final built result. Construction drawings, structural calculations, safety decisions, and permits require appropriate qualified professionals and local approval.

## 4. The 2D–6D Workflow

The meanings of “4D”, “5D”, and “6D” can differ between software and industry standards. For SNK Architect, define these terms clearly as product modules instead of claiming one universal definition.

### 2D — Floor Plan
**Purpose:** Show the layout from above.

Possible features:
- Room outlines and room names
- Dimensions and area labels
- Walls, doors, windows, stairs, and furniture layout
- Basic 2D plan preview
- Exportable image/PDF in a later version

**Limit:** An illustrative plan is not automatically a permit-ready or construction-ready drawing.

### 3D — Building and Interior Model
**Purpose:** Help clients understand the form and spatial arrangement.

Possible features:
- 3D room, floor, or house model
- Furniture, doors, windows, walls, ceilings, and roof
- Materials and colours
- Interior and exterior camera views
- Orbit, pan, and zoom controls
- Photorealistic render outputs created by a suitable rendering workflow

### 4D — Time and Construction Sequence
**Purpose:** Communicate how a project may progress over time.

Possible features:
- Timeline stages: foundation, structure, walls, roof, finishes
- Stage-by-stage building assembly animation
- Milestones and planned dates
- Progress status for projects with real data

**Limit:** A visual simulation is not a validated construction schedule. Actual timing depends on site conditions, approvals, labour, weather, materials, and contractors.

### 5D — Cost and Quantity Planning
**Purpose:** Connect project scope and quantities with budget information.

Possible features:
- Preliminary quantity list
- Material categories and unit rates
- Budget estimate by room or project
- Low/medium/high budget scenarios
- Estimate assumptions and last-updated date
- Draft bill of quantities (BOQ)

**Limit:** Do not present sample or AI-inferred prices as a final quotation. Reliable estimates need measured quantities, current local rates, defined specifications, and professional review.

### 6D — Building Information and Lifecycle
**Purpose:** Organize useful building information for operation and maintenance. Some workflows use 6D for sustainability or energy data, so SNK Architect should state its chosen definition clearly.

Possible features:
- Material and equipment records
- Product specifications and warranties
- Maintenance schedule
- Energy-efficiency or sustainability information when supported by data
- Asset register and lifecycle notes
- Handover documents

**Limit:** Energy performance and sustainability claims need valid inputs and suitable analysis; do not infer certified performance from a render.

### Future View — Completed-Home Preview
**Purpose:** Help clients imagine a proposed finished space.

Possible features:
- Photorealistic exterior and interior views
- Day/night lighting comparisons
- Alternative colours and materials
- Furniture and styling variations
- Interactive 3D viewer
- Walkthrough video or guided camera path
- Before/after concept comparisons

**Limit:** The preview represents a design proposal. It cannot guarantee the exact real-world outcome.

## 10. Technical Boundaries and Safety

- Three.js is a browser-based 3D visualization library; it does not automatically create a complete BIM or architectural design system.
- AI-generated concept imagery is not a substitute for accurate CAD/BIM models.
- Do not claim that a generated model has correct structural dimensions unless it has been checked.
- Do not present estimated construction costs as guaranteed prices.
- Keep API keys on a secure server; never expose secret keys in front-end JavaScript or a public GitHub repository.
- Obtain permission to use client photos, plans, and project files.
- Label concept renders, sample budgets, and demo projects clearly.
- Have qualified professionals review construction drawings, structural details, code compliance, and safety-critical decisions.
- Check the applicable local building regulations and approval requirements before construction.
