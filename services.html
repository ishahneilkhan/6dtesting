The 2D–6D framework

SNK Architect's own explanatory categories. Industry definitions of 4D, 5D and 6D vary. These are service descriptions, not tools on this website: the site does not yet perform construction scheduling, verified quantity take-offs, energy simulation or engineering calculations.

2D

Plan

Floor plans, layouts and spatial organization.

3D

Visualize

Rooms, houses, materials and camera views. The interactive studio is a Phase 1 example.

4D

Construction sequencing or time-linked visualization concepts.

Estimated costs linked to quantities and editable rates.

6D

Energy, environmental, maintenance and lifecycle considerations.
